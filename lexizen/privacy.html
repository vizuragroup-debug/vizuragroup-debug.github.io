This Privacy Policy is effective as of May 13, 2026 and applies to all users of the LexiZen mobile application ("App"). By using LexiZen, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account & Profile Information

  • Display name and profile picture (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Email address (when signing in via third-party providers or email)
  • Third-party user ID (when using social login features)

Gameplay Data

  • Game progress, scores, and level completions across all modes (Flow, Haiku, Harmony, Spelling Bee, Tile Rush, Word Ladder)
  • In-app currency (ZenCoins) balance and transaction history
  • Sound pack purchases and preferences

Social Features

  • Friends list and friend request activity
  • Leaderboard rankings and multiplayer match history

Device & Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version (iOS / Android)
  • App version and crash/error logs
  • Audio and app preferences stored locally on your device

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your LexiZen account
  • Save and sync your game progress across devices
  • Display leaderboards and enable multiplayer matchmaking
  • Power social features like friends lists and challenges
  • Process in-app purchases and manage your ZenCoins balance
  • Deliver and personalize sound pack content
  • Improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • Send you important service notifications (not marketing spam)
We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or third-party brokers.
🔗

3. Third-Party Services

LexiZen uses trusted third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers — Backend database, user authentication, real-time features, and file storage.
  • Authentication Providers — Secure, privacy-preserving authentication via third-party login services.
  • UI & Performance Tools — Content delivery networks and services used to provide fonts and monitor app performance.
🤝

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — Only with providers listed above who help us operate the app, under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements — If required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.
  • Business transfers — In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred with appropriate notice to you.

Public leaderboard data (your display name and score) is visible to other LexiZen players. You can change your display name in your profile settings at any time.

🗃️

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the app. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal obligations.

Game progress and preference data stored locally on your device (via shared preferences) is controlled entirely by you and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

✅

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Update inaccurate or incomplete information through your profile settings.
  • Deletion — Request that we delete your account and associated data.
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format where technically feasible.
  • Opt-out — Opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact Us section.

👶

7. Children's Privacy

LexiZen is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information promptly.

🔒

8. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. LexiZen implements the following measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — authentication is handled securely via industry-standard providers
  • Strict database security policies ensure users can only access their own data
  • Profile images are stored in secured, access-controlled storage buckets
While we strive to use industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to keep your device secure.
📝

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the effective date at the top of this page
  • Notify you through the app or via the email associated with your account

Your continued use of LexiZen after any changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
